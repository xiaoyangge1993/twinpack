Twinpack 生成的是商业发票和装箱单（commercial invoice / packing list）草稿。
它不是报关单、报关委托，也不是中国增值税发票或任何税务机关出具或认可的发票。
本工具不计算应纳税额或关税，不对 HS 编码做归类，不构成法律、税务或海关合规建议。
贸易术语、原产国、港口、重量、体积和编码均由使用者自行填写，仅用于展示。
所有单据数据只保存在使用者的浏览器中，不会上传到服务器。

Twinpack produces commercial invoice and packing list drafts.
It is not a customs declaration, not a China VAT invoice, and is not issued or endorsed by any tax or customs authority.
It does not calculate tax or duty, does not classify HS codes, and does not constitute legal, tax, or customs-compliance advice.
Incoterms, origin, ports, weights, volume, and codes are entered by the user and shown for document purposes only.
Document data stays in the user's browser and is not uploaded to a server.
